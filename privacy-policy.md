Society Connect – Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 22, 2026

1. Introduction

Society Connect helps housing societies manage residents, flats, maintenance bills, payments, notices, complaints, and visitor-related activities. This Privacy Policy explains how information may be collected, used, and protected when you use our application.

2. Contact Information

For privacy-related questions, contact us at:

Email: Societyconnect001@gmail.com

3. Information We May Collect

Depending on the features you use, Society Connect may process:

- Account details, such as email address and authentication information.
- Resident and flat details entered by the society or its authorised users.
- Maintenance bills, payment status, receipts, and transaction references.
- Notices, complaints, and visitor check-in information.
- Device and notification information needed to deliver app notifications.
- Technical information required to maintain app security and reliability.

Please do not enter information that is unnecessary for the app's intended purpose.

4. How We Use Information

Information may be used to:

- Create and manage user accounts.
- Support society and resident management.
- Display maintenance bills and payment records.
- Deliver notices and relevant notifications.
- Manage complaints and visitor records.
- Maintain security, troubleshoot issues, and improve the service.

5. Storage and Third-Party Services

The app may use Google Firebase services for authentication, database storage, and notifications. If online payments are enabled, payment processing may be handled by Razorpay or another configured payment provider.

These providers process information according to their own terms and privacy policies. Society Connect should not store or request your complete card PIN, UPI PIN, or banking password.

6. Sharing of Information

Information may be accessible to authorised society administrators and users when necessary for society operations. Information may also be processed by service providers supporting the app, or disclosed where required by law. We do not claim to sell users' personal information.

7. Data Security

We use appropriate technical and organisational measures intended to protect information. However, no electronic storage or transmission method can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

8. Data Retention and Deletion

Information should be retained only as long as necessary for the app's purposes and applicable legal requirements. To request access, correction, or deletion of your information, contact Societyconnect001@gmail.com. We may need to verify your identity and coordinate with the relevant society administrator. Some records may need to be retained where required by law.

9. Children's Privacy

Society Connect is designed for housing society management, not specifically for children. Parents or guardians should contact us if they believe a child has provided personal information inappropriately.

10. Your Choices

You should provide accurate information and use the app only for authorised purposes. You may contact us to raise privacy concerns or request correction or deletion, subject to applicable legal and operational requirements.

11. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when our app or practices change. The updated version will be published on this page with a revised update date.
