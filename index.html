Privacy Policy

Society Connect helps housing societies manage residents, flats, maintenance bills, payments, notices, complaints and visitors.

Depending on the features used, the app may process account details, resident and flat details, maintenance records, payment status, notices, complaints and notification information.

Information is used to provide app features, maintain security, manage society operations and support users. Information may be shared with service providers when needed to operate app features.

Users should only submit information they are authorized to provide. Please contact us to request help with your personal information.
